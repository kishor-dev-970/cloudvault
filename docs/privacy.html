CloudVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CloudVault is a zero-server mobile application. It does not run, rent, or transmit data through any server operated by the developer.

Data the app handles

  • Your Google account connection: With your permission, the app requests OAuth access to upload videos to your own YouTube channel and manage a private playlist. Tokens are stored only in the app's secure, on-device storage on your phone.
  • Your videos: When you choose to upload a video, it is streamed directly from your device to YouTube using YouTube's upload API. A local thumbnail of the video may be stored in the app's on-device cache for display in your library.

What is not collected

The app has no servers, analytics, or advertising. It does not collect, log, or share any personal information, video content, or usage data with third parties.

Data retention and control

Videos live in your own private YouTube channel and are subject to YouTube's terms. You can delete any video from within YouTube or from the app. Disconnecting the app in Settings removes its stored authentication tokens from your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CloudVault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
